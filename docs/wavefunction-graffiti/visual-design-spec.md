# Wavefunction Graffiti Visual Design Specification

## Creative Direction

Wavefunction Graffiti should feel like a cross between:

- a high-end scientific instrument
- a cinematic creative-coding piece
- a macOS-native pro audio interface

The visuals should be restrained, luminous, and precise.

## Visual Keywords

- dark glass
- spectral glow
- brushed-metal restraint
- oscilloscope elegance
- fluid motion
- scientific calm

## Palette

Base colors:

- `obsidian`: deep blue-black for the background
- `graphite`: softened card surfaces
- `frost`: translucent glass borders

Signal colors:

- `cyan`: primary amplitude traces
- `teal`: density fills and positive-energy accents
- `amber`: uncertainty and warning emphasis
- `coral`: secondary evolution accents

Avoid neon overload. The interface should glow selectively against a dark field rather than saturate the whole screen.

## Typography

- Primary UI font: geometric sans with personality, such as Space Grotesk
- Numeric and scientific labels: IBM Plex Mono or similar
- Large title treatment: cinematic tracking, light weight, high contrast

Typography should feel authored, not default.

## Layout Language

- Large central canvases dominate the viewport.
- Controls live in compact glass docks rather than sidebars full of forms.
- Cards use rounded corners, translucent backgrounds, and subtle edge lighting.
- Spacing should feel roomy and intentional.

## Panel Design

Each visualization panel contains:

- title and short descriptor
- primary canvas region
- compact legend or mode badge
- optional annotation line

The position and momentum panels should feel like paired instruments from the same lab bench.

## Motion

- smooth panel reveals
- subtle cursor-follow highlights
- eased line interpolation
- restrained trail effects during evolution
- transport animations that suggest scientific playback, not entertainment UI

Animations should support understanding, not distract from it.

## Graph Style

- thin luminous traces
- soft filled densities
- understated grid lines
- phase encoded with hue variation or a spectral strip, not rainbow chaos
- uncertainty values emphasized with elegant numeric cards

Avoid stock chart axes and heavy boxed plots.

## Educational Overlays

Overlays should be concise floating notes with:

- one strong sentence
- one equation or symbol line if useful
- a visual cue tied to the panel

They should feel like whispered guidance, not modal tutorials.

## Accessibility and Legibility

- Maintain sufficient contrast for text and key traces.
- Use color plus shape or labeling for important distinctions.
- Keep pointer targets comfortable on laptop trackpads.
- Preserve clarity on retina and standard-density displays.

## Anti-Goals

- no bright classroom primary colors
- no spreadsheet-like controls
- no textbook beige or white panels
- no generic startup landing-page styling
- no overdesigned pseudo-3D skeuomorphism
